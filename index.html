Main source: modelmetrics.net · Zenless Zone Zero · Female Character Measurements. Schema v2 · Data updated 2026-10-25.
Height: the charted figure may include ears, footwear or accessories. Height uses head height where the chart notes an appendage (Miyabi: 170 cm charted, 152.1 cm used); the charted figure stays visible as a note.
Basis: cards, filters and rankings use the standard model, except Jane Doe, whose canonical record is Nocturne of Light. Other measured outfits are separate records with their own deltas; art-only outfits never touch a number.
Classes: waist (Cinched / Average / Fuller), hips (Compact / Average-width / Wide) and thighs (Slim / Average / Thick) are roster terciles of the raw circumference in cm. Build compares raw bust and hip percentiles; the cup group follows the source UK cup letter. Thirteen agents are hand-pinned to Thick by curator read. Shape uses bust, waist and hips: Hourglass needs bust/waist and hip/waist ≥1.45 and a bust–hip balance within 15%; Straight is a flare below 1.30 or a mismatch over 25%; the rest is Balanced.
Percentile bars: roster percentiles of the raw cm measurement; height percentiles use head height. 0 = smallest, 100 = largest.
Bust diff: recomputed as bust minus under-bust so it keeps one decimal (the sheet's own diff column is whole-number rounded).
